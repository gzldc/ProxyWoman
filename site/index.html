Free, open-source HTTP/HTTPS debugging proxy for Mac

ProxyWoman captures, decrypts, inspects, mocks and rewrites network traffic from your Mac apps, browsers, iPhone, Android and the iOS Simulator. Native Swift, free forever, no account, no telemetry.

Download for macOS Source on GitHub

macOS 14 or later · Apple silicon and Intel · Apache-2.0 · Version 0.1.3

Features

HTTPS, HTTP/2, WebSocket

Decrypt HTTPS with a root certificate generated on your Mac. HTTP/1.1, HTTP/2 and WebSocket messages, grouped by app and host.

Rules, Map Local, Map Remote

Unlimited rules with field conditions and ordered actions: rewrite headers, bodies, URLs and status codes, return mock responses or local files, delay or drop requests.

Breakpoints

Pause a request before it is sent or a response before it arrives, edit it, then continue or abort.

Compose and repeat

Build or edit a request, send it, repeat it with a count, concurrency and interval.

HAR import and export

Save sessions as HAR and open them again. Opens HAR files from Chrome, Safari, Firefox, Charles and Proxyman. Copy any request as cURL.

JWT decoding

Tokens in headers, cookies and URLs are decoded automatically, with issue and expiry times.

iPhone, Android, iOS Simulator

Phone setup with a QR code and a certificate download page. One click installs the certificate into every running iOS Simulator.

Safe system proxy

Turns the macOS system proxy on and off, restores your settings even after a crash, and chains to Clash or another existing proxy.

MCP server for AI

Claude, Cursor and other MCP clients can read and analyse captured traffic, with sensitive values redacted by default.

English and Chinese

Full English and Simplified Chinese interfaces, switchable in the app.

Quick start

  1. Download the DMG for your Mac (-arm for Apple silicon, -intel for Intel) and drag ProxyWoman to Applications.
  2. The app is not notarized yet: on first launch open System Settings ▸ Privacy & Security ▸ Open Anyway.
  3. Choose Certificate ▸ Install Certificate on This Mac… to read HTTPS.
  4. Turn on System Proxy in the toolbar, then use your app or browser.

FAQ

Is ProxyWoman free?
Yes. Every feature is free for personal and commercial use under the Apache License 2.0. No paid tier, trial, account or analytics.
What is a good free alternative to Proxyman or Charles on Mac?
ProxyWoman: HTTPS decryption, unlimited rules, Map Local, Map Remote, breakpoints, Compose, HAR import and export, phone and iOS Simulator capture and an MCP server, all free and open source.
How is it different from Wireshark?
Wireshark records raw packets and HTTPS stays encrypted without session keys. ProxyWoman works at the HTTP level, decrypts HTTPS as a proxy and can change requests and responses.
Can it capture iPhone or Android traffic?
Yes. Turn on Allow devices on the local network, set the phone's Wi-Fi proxy to the address shown, and download the certificate from http://proxywoman.cert or the QR code.
Can it capture the iOS Simulator?
Yes. Boot the simulator, choose Certificate ▸ Install Certificate on iOS Simulator, then turn on System Proxy.
Will I lose internet access while capturing?
No. ProxyWoman chains to an existing proxy such as Clash and restores your original settings when you turn it off or quit, even after a crash.
Does it upload my traffic?
No. Captured sessions stay on your Mac.
